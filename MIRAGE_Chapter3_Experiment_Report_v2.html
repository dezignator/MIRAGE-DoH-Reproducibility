MIRAGE-DoH Chapter 3: experiments, attack scenarios and performance (v2 artifact)

Every number was produced by the scripts in this repository on the machine described at the end. Traffic is synthetic (mirage_v2/traffic_v2.py), baselines are scikit-learn models rather than the thesis CNN-LSTM, cryptography runs on a CPU, and Classic McEliece is represented by a byte-size model only. These are not the values of Tables 8-12; they show how each mechanism behaves under controlled conditions and where the thesis figures depend on hardware or testbed data not in this repository.
